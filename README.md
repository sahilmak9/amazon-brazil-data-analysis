# 📊 Strategic Insights from Amazon Brazil for Amazon India

An end-to-end SQL business analysis project using the **Amazon Brazil E-commerce Dataset**. The project analyzes customer behavior, payment trends, product performance, and sales patterns using SQL and presents business insights and recommendations for Amazon India.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Analyze Amazon Brazil's e-commerce data to identify customer behavior, sales trends, and operational insights that can support business decisions for Amazon India.

---

## 🗂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Amazon Brazil E-commerce Dataset

The project uses the following tables:

- Customers
- Orders
- Order Items
- Payments
- Products
- Sellers

- Payment Consistency Analysis
- Missing Product Category Analysis
- Order Value Segmentation
- Customer Classification
- Top Revenue Categories
- Seasonal Sales Analysis
- Product Sales Analysis
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Loyalty Segmentation
- Top Customers by Average Order Value
- Monthly Cumulative Sales
- Month-over-Month Growth by Payment Type

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Credit cards account for the highest share of transactions.
- Most customers are occasional buyers, indicating an opportunity to improve customer retention.
- Health & Beauty and Gift categories generate the highest revenue.
- Spring and Summer contribute the highest overall sales.
- Window Functions helped analyze customer rankings and month-over-month sales trends.
